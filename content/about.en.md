Harper keeps a record of live music, moving between Shanghai and Seattle.

Drawn to jazz, experimental sound, and the moment a connection forms between people and what they hear. Writes about shows, practice sessions, and ideas that have not settled into shape.

*Harper's Live Sessions* is the continuing archive of that work, with no fixed end point: some of it will be finished, some will keep changing, some may stay in exploration for good.

But keeping the record is part of the work.
