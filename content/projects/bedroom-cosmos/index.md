Bedroom Cosmos begins with a small premise: a room can be large enough to hold an entire night sky when attention is close enough.

The project is being shaped as a live session rather than a polished studio document. Breath, hesitation, room tone, and the distance between notes are part of the arrangement.

## Current form

The piece is still in rehearsal. Its structure is deliberately loose, with a few melodic landmarks and open passages for improvisation.

Media, credits, and session details will be added as the work is documented.
