WU 雾 is a flutist and improviser who has taken part in jazz ensembles, live performances, and cross-disciplinary improvisation. Current practice explores flute, shoegaze, improvisation, and sound texture, with a first flute-led shoegaze EP taking shape in Logic Pro.

Harper Live Sessions holds music writing, observations from live shows, and reflections on practice. Some entries come from the stage and rehearsal room, others from listening in the audience. Writing and playing meet here.
