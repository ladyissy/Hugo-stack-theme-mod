Harper’s Live Sessions is a personal archive that keeps growing.

It holds live music, listening notes, practice sessions, and ideas that have not settled into shape.

Some of it comes from the stage, some from the rehearsal room, some from a chance encounter.

None of it is necessarily finished, but all of it carries the trace of something that happened.

## Why keep a record?

The moment music happens is always brief.

Once a set ends, the sound goes, the lights go down, the audience leaves.

But the feelings, the questions and the ideas that arrived in that moment are worth keeping.

This space is not here to present a finished result.

It is more interested in the process:

how a practice session turns into a piece,\
how watching one show changes the way music is understood,\
how a live room connects the people making the sound to the people hearing it.
{.statement__passage}

## What is kept here

Field Notes
: Observations on shows, festivals, artists, and the working process.

Sessions
: Records of performances, collaborations, and experiments with sound.

Practice
: Instrument study, technical exploration, and the daily work of becoming a musician.

Works in Progress
: Pieces and ideas that are still taking shape.

## About the editor

Harper’s Live Sessions was started by Harper.

Currently moving between Shanghai and Seattle.

Interested in live music, jazz, experimental sound, and the moment a connection forms between people and what they hear.

There is no fixed end point here.

Some work will be finished,\
some will keep changing,\
some may stay in exploration for good.
{.statement__passage}

But keeping the record is part of the work.
